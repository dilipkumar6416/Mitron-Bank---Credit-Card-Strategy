## 🔑 Key Insights & Strategic Recommendations

- *Customer Preferences*: Young adults prefer digital payments; Gen X leans toward EMIs and cards.
- *Income Insights*: Mumbai tops in earnings; IT professionals are high-income, high-spend customers.
- *Spending Behavior*: Peak spending happens mid-month; preferred categories vary by occupation.
- *Income Utilization: IT sector customers use up to **90%* of their monthly income — a key segment for high-limit cards.

### Recommended Actions:

1. *Segmented Credit Cards*: Tailor card offerings for different age, income, and occupation groups.
2. *Target IT Professionals*: Offer high-reward, premium cards for high-income, high-utilization users.
3. *Seasonal Promotions*: Align marketing with spending peaks (e.g., 15th–25th of each month).
4. *City-Specific Campaigns*: Focus efforts in cities like Mumbai and Hyderabad with strong spending trends.
